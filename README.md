# Overview

Gujarat Art & Craft ERP is an enterprise-grade manufacturing ERP designed specifically for businesses dealing with production, inventory, invoicing and finance.

Unlike traditional invoice software, this ERP manages the complete manufacturing lifecycle:

Raw Material Purchase

↓

Production Planning

↓

Job Work

↓

Collections

↓

Finished Goods

↓

Inventory

↓

Sales

↓

Finance

↓

Reports

↓

Business Analytics

The architecture is designed to scale from a single manufacturing unit to a multi-branch enterprise.
